One picture can mislead our intuition.

These 20 dates were chosen to be spread out. This is an illustration, not a random sample or an estimate of the probability.

For 20 independent, uniformly distributed birthdays, ignoring leap years, there are 190 pairs that could match. The probability of at least one shared birthday is about 41%.
